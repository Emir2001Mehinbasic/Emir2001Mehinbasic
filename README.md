### 🙋 A bit about me

I'm a computer science grad who'd rather be building something than just reading about it. Most days I'm somewhere between shipping a small web app, fighting with a Dockerfile until it finally builds, or explaining to a 10-year-old why their Scratch sprite won't move.

I started out doing freelance/agency-style web work (WordPress, responsive sites, the usual), and these days I split my time between full-stack projects and teaching. I currently work as a **Junior Instructor at STEM Akademija**, where I run hands-on sessions in Python, JavaScript, and robotics (mBot, LEGO WeDo, Micro:bit) for kids — it's chaotic in the best way, and it's taught me more about explaining ideas clearly than any course did.

I like backend work the most — APIs, databases, the "how does this actually hold together" layer — but I'm comfortable across the stack, and I've been getting deeper into Docker/Kubernetes lately because I like understanding how my code actually gets deployed, not just that it runs on `localhost`.

### 🧪 What I'm into right now

- Building small backend services properly — auth, rate-limiting, background jobs, not just CRUD
- Containerizing everything I build, even side projects, just for the practice
- Picking up Go and sharpening my C# — TypeScript/Node are home base, but I like not being a one-language person
- Figuring out where AI tools genuinely speed me up vs. where they just feel fast
